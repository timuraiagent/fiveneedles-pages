Five Needles — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Camera

Five Needles uses the camera to find your hand and show where an acupressure point is. Camera frames are processed on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Video and images are not recorded or stored, and nothing leaves your phone unless you send a problem report.

Problem reports

If the marker is in the wrong place, you can tap Report a problem. Only then, and only for that report, the app sends to our server: the current camera frame, a screenshot with the markers, the detected hand joints and markers, the selected point and hand, your device model and iOS version, and the note you type. Reports have no account, name or device identifier attached. We use them only to improve point placement, do not share them, and do not use them for tracking or advertising. To have a report deleted, email us.

Other data

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Apart from problem reports it makes no network requests. Your settings are stored only on your device.

Permissions

The only permission is camera access. Without it the camera view does not work; the rest of the app does.

Deleting your data

All app data on your phone is removed when you delete the app. For reports you have sent, email info@0xffsec.ca.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data (for example reminders or new content from a server), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
