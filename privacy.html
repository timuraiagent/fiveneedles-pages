Five Needles — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Camera

Five Needles uses the camera to find your hand and show where an acupressure point is. Camera frames are processed only on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Video and images are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it makes no network requests. Your settings and progress are stored only on your device.

Permissions

The only permission is camera access. Without it the camera view does not work; the rest of the app does.

Deleting your data

All app data is removed when you delete the app.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data (for example reminders or new content from a server), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
