Five Needles — Support

What it is

A free daily acupressure ritual: five points on the hands and legs, about a minute each, plus a library of points for everyday needs such as sleep, tension and a stiff neck at the desk. The camera can show where a point is on your hand. Five Needles is a wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a health professional.

How to use it

You need an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No sign-in is needed. Follow the daily ritual, or pick a point in the library and point the camera at your hand to see where it is.
